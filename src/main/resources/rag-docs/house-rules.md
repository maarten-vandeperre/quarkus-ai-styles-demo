# The Quarkus Tap - House Rules

1. Happy hour runs Monday to Thursday from 17:00 to 19:00: 20% off all signature cocktails.
2. Last orders at 00:30, doors close at 01:00.
3. Table reservations of 6 people or more require a deposit of EUR 10 per person.
4. Dogs are welcome on the terrace, not inside.
5. Corkage fee for bring-your-own wine: EUR 15 per bottle, maximum two bottles per table.
6. Allergen information for every cocktail is available at the bar; all syrups are gluten-free.
7. Payment: card only, no cash.
